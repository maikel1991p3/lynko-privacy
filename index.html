LYNKO

Lynko works without an account and without internet.

No accounts, no servers of our own

There is no sign-up, and Lynko has no server of its own. The whole game works offline.

What is saved, and where

Your progress, your settings and your hints are saved on this device. If you use Google Play Games, a copy of your progress (not your settings) is also kept in your Play Games cloud, so you can get it back on another phone. You can delete your progress yourself from Settings.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android, if you use Google Play Games, Lynko uses it for achievements, leaderboards and cloud saves. Google manages your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), your scores, your achievements and your saved progress under its own privacy policy. Lynko never asks for your email or password and does not store them. If you do not use Play Games, everything stays on your phone.

Permissions

Lynko asks for no sensitive permission: no camera, no microphone, no contacts, no location and no files. It uses vibration and, for the optional Google Play Games features, internet access; Android does not ask you to approve either of them. If you turn on reminders, Android asks for your permission to show notifications. They are created on your device, without internet, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Ads and purchases

There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Suitable for all ages

Lynko is a puzzle game with no violence, no inappropriate language and no adult content. There is no chat and no way for one player to contact another. If you use Google Play Games, leaderboards only show your Play Games gamer name.

Contact

If you report a problem, Lynko opens your email app with the message already written: the app version, your phone’s system version and, if there was an error, its technical details. You read it and decide whether to send it; the app sends nothing by itself.
